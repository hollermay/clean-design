# Three presets (use when the user has no taste or reference yet)

Copy the values into the brief. Each uses one accent color and fonts that are free to use (Google Fonts).

## A. Plain editorial (writing, portfolios, newsletters)
- Background `#FAF8F4`, text `#1B1B1B`, muted `#6B6B6B`, accent `#B4442B`
- Heading: Fraunces 600. Body: Source Serif 4 400, 18 px, line-height 1.6
- Layout: one narrow column (max 640 px), generous top space (96 px), no cards, rules instead of boxes
- Corner radius 2 px, no shadows

## B. Dense utility (dev tools, dashboards, docs)
- Background `#0F1115`, panel `#171A21`, text `#E6E8EE`, muted `#8B93A5`, accent `#4DA3FF`
- Heading and body: IBM Plex Sans 400 and 600. Code: IBM Plex Mono
- Layout: left-aligned, 12-column grid, tables and lists over cards, 8 px base spacing
- Corner radius 4 px, 1 px borders instead of shadows

## C. Warm product (consumer apps, small shops)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, surface `#F5F1EA`, text `#222222`, muted `#6F6A60`, accent `#1F7A5A`
- Heading: DM Sans 700. Body: DM Sans 400, 17 px
- Layout: big real product photo at the top, one sentence, one button; features as a plain list of 4 lines
- Corner radius 12 px (everywhere), one soft shadow level

Rule: pick ONE preset and do not mix. Change the accent only if the brand already has a color.
